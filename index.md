# Gamma Systems Research Laboratory

Welcome to the official repository of **Gamma Systems Research Laboratory (GSRL)**.

## Primary Research Areas

* **Robust Control Theory & Stability Analysis:** Focus on interval matrices, spectral bounds, and closed-loop robustness margins.
* **Hermitian Spectral Bound (HSB) & Spectral Control Operators (OSC).**
* **Applied Dynamical Systems:** Advanced control applications in satellite attitude dynamics and hybrid microgrids.
